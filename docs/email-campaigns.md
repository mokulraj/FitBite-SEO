# FitBite Email Campaigns

All campaign content is portfolio/demo content. No real recipients or results are claimed.

## 1. Welcome Email
- Subject: Welcome to FitBite — your healthier routine starts here
- Preview: Here’s what to expect from FitBite and your first-meal offer.
- Audience: New subscribers
- Objective: Introduce FitBite and set expectations.
- CTA: Explore FitBite

## 2. Educational Email
- Subject: 3 simple ways to make weekday lunches healthier
- Preview: Small meal-planning changes can make healthy choices easier.
- Audience: New subscribers
- Objective: Provide useful content before a hard promotional ask.
- CTA: Read the lunch guide

## 3. Promotional Email
- Subject A: Get 20% Off Your First Healthy Meal
- Subject B: Eat Healthy Today — Save 20%
- Preview: A simple first-meal offer for the FitBite campaign.
- Audience: Interested users
- Objective: Drive qualified traffic to the campaign landing page.
- CTA: Claim 20% Off

## 4. Follow-up Email
- Subject: Still thinking about your first healthy meal?
- Preview: A reminder with a clear next step and useful content.
- Audience: Interested users who did not convert.
- Objective: Recover intent without adding pressure.
- CTA: See the offer

## 5. Newsletter
- Subject: FitBite Weekly: healthy habits + new ideas
- Preview: One useful article, one practical tip and one campaign CTA.
- Audience: Customers / engaged subscribers
- Objective: Maintain engagement and encourage repeat action.
- CTA: Read this week’s guide

## Segmentation
- New subscribers → Welcome + educational sequence
- Interested users → Promotional + follow-up sequence
- Customers → Newsletter / loyalty content
- Inactive users → Re-engagement email after a defined inactivity window
