# FitBite Campaign Report

## Executive summary
FitBite is a fictional local healthy-meal delivery concept used to demonstrate an integrated SEO and email lead-generation campaign. The campaign connects keyword intent, landing-page optimization, consent-based lead capture, email nurturing and analytics.

## Funnel
Traffic → Website Visit → Campaign Landing Page → Lead Form → Email Signup → Welcome Email → Promotional Email → Website Visit → Conversion

## Measurement plan
Primary KPI: lead conversion rate.
Supporting KPIs: landing-page conversion rate, CTA click rate, email open rate, email CTR, unsubscribe rate, source-level traffic and conversion.

## A/B test
Version A: “Get 20% Off Your First Healthy Meal”
Version B: “Eat Healthy Today — Save 20%”
Primary winner metric: conversion rate. Secondary diagnostics: open rate, CTR and form completion.

## Data integrity
Dashboard figures are simulated/demo data. They are not real customer results, rankings, email results or business performance.
