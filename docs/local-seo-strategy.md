# FitBite Local SEO Strategy

## Business
FitBite — fictional healthy food / meal delivery portfolio business.

## Service area
Use one selected local city in Tamil Nadu for the portfolio story. The website currently uses Madurai as the demo city in forms, but no real service coverage is claimed.

## Local keyword themes
- healthy food delivery near me
- healthy meal delivery in [city]
- healthy lunch delivery in [city]
- diet food delivery in [city]
- fitness meal delivery in [city]

No search volume is claimed without verification in Google Keyword Planner or another keyword tool.

## Google Business Profile strategy
If this were a real business, create/verify a legitimate profile, select the correct business category, maintain accurate contact/service-area information, add real photos and respond to reviews. This project does not claim a live profile.

## Local landing page
Create `/healthy-food-delivery-[city]/` only when the business genuinely serves that location. Match the page to local intent and add unique, useful information rather than doorway pages.

## Review strategy
After a genuine customer interaction, ask for an honest review. Do not incentivize or manufacture reviews.
