# FitBite Social Media Strategy

## Instagram
- Frequency: 3 posts + 2 stories per week (plan)
- Content: meal visuals, healthy lunch tips, behind-the-scenes concepts, blog snippets
- Audience: young professionals and fitness beginners
- CTA: Visit the first-meal campaign page
- Hashtags: combine broad, local and intent-based tags; validate final tags before use

## Facebook
- Frequency: 3 posts per week (plan)
- Content: blog articles, meal plan explainers, local community content
- Audience: local households and working adults
- CTA: Read the guide / Get Started
- Hashtags: use sparingly; prioritize clear copy and local relevance

## LinkedIn
- Frequency: 2 posts per week (plan)
- Content: workplace lunch habits, productivity-friendly food routines, campaign case-study insights
- Audience: professionals and HR/workplace audiences
- CTA: Read the healthy lunch article
- Hashtags: focus on relevant professional topics rather than volume

No real social accounts, engagement or followers are claimed.
