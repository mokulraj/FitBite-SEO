# FitBite — SEO & Email Marketing Lead Generation Campaign

> A complete digital marketing portfolio project for a fictional local healthy-meal business, built to demonstrate SEO, lead generation, email marketing, landing-page optimization, content marketing, analytics, and conversion optimization.

**FitBite is a fictional portfolio business. All marketing performance numbers shown in the dashboard and campaign report are simulated/demo data. No real customers, leads, rankings, conversions, email campaign results, or Google Business Profile results are claimed.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

FitBite is a portfolio project that combines **digital marketing strategy with a working Django web application**.

The project demonstrates how a marketer can plan and implement a lead-generation campaign for a local food business, from:

**SEO research → landing page → lead capture → segmentation → email funnel → analytics → campaign reporting → conversion optimization**

The website is built as a realistic business website rather than a simple blog.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The fictional business, FitBite, provides convenient healthy meal options for busy people.

The campaign focuses on:

- Increasing organic search visibility
- Attracting relevant website visitors
- Converting visitors into leads
- Capturing marketing consent
- Segmenting potential customers
- Building an email nurture funnel
- Improving landing-page conversions
- Measuring marketing performance
- Creating a repeatable digital marketing process

---

## 👥 Target Audience

The campaign focuses on people who are interested in convenient and healthier meal options.

Example audience groups include:

1. **Busy professionals**
2. **Fitness-focused customers**
3. **Health-conscious customers**
4. **People looking for convenient meal plans**

These segments are used throughout the lead-generation and email-marketing strategy.
