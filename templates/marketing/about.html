ABOUT THE PROJECT

FitBite is a fictional business built like a real marketing campaign.

It is designed to demonstrate how SEO, content, lead generation, email marketing and analytics can work together.

Business concept

FitBite is a healthy food / meal delivery concept targeting customers in a selected local city in Tamil Nadu. The project intentionally avoids claiming real customers, rankings, campaign results or a real Google Business Profile.

Target audiences

  • Busy professionals seeking convenient lunch options
  • Fitness beginners exploring balanced meal routines
  • People interested in practical diet-friendly meals
  • Local customers comparing healthy food delivery options
MARKETING OBJECTIVE

Turn relevant traffic into consent-based leads.

Primary conversion: lead signup for the first-meal offer. Secondary conversion: newsletter subscription and content engagement.
