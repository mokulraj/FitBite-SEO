CASE STUDY

SEO & Email Marketing Lead Generation Campaign

A portfolio-ready campaign narrative you can walk through in an interview.

01

Business objective

Generate qualified, consent-based leads from relevant local search and content traffic, then nurture them through a short email funnel toward a first-order action.

02

Target audience

Busy professionals, fitness beginners and health-conscious local customers comparing convenient meal options.

03

SEO strategy

Map primary, secondary and long-tail keywords to home, service, blog and campaign pages. Use clear titles, descriptions, heading hierarchy, internal links, canonical URLs, sitemap and robots.txt.

04

Content strategy

Publish useful articles around healthy lunches, balanced diets, meal planning, delivery-service evaluation and fitness meals. Every article includes a CTA.

05

Lead-generation strategy

Use a focused landing page, short form, consent checkbox, clear value proposition and UTM capture. Store leads in Django and prevent duplicate email addresses.

06

Email funnel

Welcome → Education → Promotion → Follow-up → Newsletter. Segment new subscribers, interested users, customers and inactive users.

07

A/B testing

Test “Get 20% Off Your First Healthy Meal” against “Eat Healthy Today — Save 20%”. Primary decision metric: conversion rate; supporting metrics: open rate and CTR.

08

Analytics

Track website visitors, traffic source, landing-page visits, form submissions, CTA clicks, email clicks and conversions. GA4/Search Console fields are environment-driven and optional.

09

Problems identified

Generic food pages can have weak intent matching; long forms can reduce completion; disconnected email and landing-page messaging can reduce continuity.

10

Optimization suggestions

Build city-specific content, test headline/CTA combinations, improve page speed, segment by intent, add high-value email content and review funnel drop-offs.

11

Future strategy

Connect Brevo or Mailchimp, configure real SMTP, import consented lists, connect GA4 and Search Console, run controlled A/B tests and replace demo metrics with verified campaign data.

IMPORTANT: No rankings, customers, conversions, email results, social engagement or Google Business Profile are claimed by this portfolio project.
